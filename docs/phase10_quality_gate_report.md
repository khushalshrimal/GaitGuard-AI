# GaitGuard AI — Phase 10 Execution & Validation Report

## Executive Summary
Phase 10 of **GaitGuard AI** has successfully integrated the **Video Quality Gate, Capture Coach, and System Integration Layer**. 

The Video Quality Gate evaluates video duration, resolution, keypoint trajectory coverage, walking motion displacement, motion blur, and framing occupancy prior to executing Phase 7 BiLSTM inference. When actionable quality defects occur (e.g. static cow, motion blur, severe occlusion, short duration), the pipeline intercepts execution and outputs a `RETRY` status alongside human-readable guidance generated by the **Capture Coach**.

All 20 unit tests in `tests/test_phase10_quality.py` passed cleanly, and full regression testing across Phase 2 through Phase 10 (106 total tests) succeeded with zero regressions.

## Capture Coach Guidance Examples

1. **Short Video (`VIDEO_TOO_SHORT`)**:
   > *"Video is too short. Record a slightly longer walking sequence (at least 3-5 seconds)."*

2. **Stationary Cow (`INSUFFICIENT_WALKING`)**:
   > *"Cow is standing still or moving too slowly. Ensure the cow is walking continuously in a straight line."*

3. **Motion Blur (`EXCESSIVE_BLUR`)**:
   > *"Hold the camera steady and ensure adequate lighting to reduce motion blur."*

4. **Occlusion / Low Coverage (`LOW_KEYPOINT_COVERAGE`)**:
   > *"Cow keypoints are partially hidden. Ensure full side view of cow without barriers or fences blocking the view."*
