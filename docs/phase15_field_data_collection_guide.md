# GaitGuard AI — Phase 15 Field Data Collection Guide

## 1. Overview
This guide provides field operators and data collectors with guidelines for capturing cattle walking videos for GaitGuard AI screening and validation datasets.

## 2. Project Capture Guidance (Not Veterinary Standards)
- **Camera Viewpoint**: Parallel side view ($90^\circ$ perpendicular to cow walk path).
- **Camera Distance**: 4 to 6 meters away from the walking corridor.
- **Walking Segment**: Continuous lateral walk of at least 3 to 5 full stride cycles (duration: 3–8 seconds).
- **Surface**: Flat, non-slippery concrete or rubber walkway with minimal mud accumulation.
- **Lighting**: Adequate daylight or uniform indoor barn illumination avoiding harsh shadows.

## 3. Metadata Recording Checklist
For every captured video, log:
- Cow ear tag / animal ID (`animal_id`)
- Recording date & time
- Recording site (`site_id`)
- Camera type & resolution
- Independent Sprecher score assessment (if evaluator present)
